Ask the company a question. Get the answer with a source trail.

CortexHQ is a workspace for rehearsing company memory: pick sample sources, paste internal notes, and ask what was decided. It returns a structured answer, source cards, open decisions with owners, a memory map, and a timeline. It does not connect to your real tools.

What this is not

  • Not a live Slack, email, or CRM connector.
  • Not a production knowledge base of a real company.

The product exists so operators can practice asking, citing, and assigning — on sample notes they paste themselves.
